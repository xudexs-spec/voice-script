口播声纹 · voice-script
从你脑子里一闪而过的念头，到一篇能直接对着提词器念的口播稿——全程用你自己的话，不是 AI 腔。
From a fleeting thought in your head to a teleprompter-ready script — in your words, not AI's.
解决什么痛点 · The pain it solves
① 用 AI 抄的观点，注定没数据。
现在越来越多的人用 AI 写稿、做文案——但念出来的视频没数据。为什么？因为那些观点、那些感悟，不是他自己的，只是抄了别人的。
只有当你真正遇到过这件事、真正想过这个问题，你才会有感触，讲出来的话才有情感，才打得动人。AI 再聪明，也写不出你的真实经历和感触——这正是 AI 和人之间最根本的区别。
② 念头，转瞬即逝。
人每天产生 6000 多个念头（每分钟约 6.5 个，2020 年皇后大学研究）。绝大多数一闪而过——当下不记，就再也想不起来。而这些念头里，藏着只有你才有的内容。丢了，就是永久丢了。
① Copied ideas get no traction.
More and more people use AI to write scripts and copy — but the videos they record get no traction. Why? Because those opinions and insights aren't theirs; they just copied someone else's.
Only when you've truly lived it and truly thought about it do you feel it — and only then can your words carry emotion and move people. AI, no matter how smart, can't write your real experience and feeling. That's the fundamental difference between AI and people.
② Thoughts — gone in a flash.
You have 6,000+ thoughts a day (about 6.5 per minute; Queen's University, 2020). Most vanish the moment they appear — if you don't capture them now, they're gone forever. And those thoughts hold content only you can make.

它的立场 · Its stance
AI 不当你的替身，当你的整理工。 你说的话是原料，AI 只负责把它理成能念的稿。那股"人味儿"，永远是你自己的。
AI is not your ghostwriter; it's your editor. You speak; it tidies. The "human" in it stays yours.
它凭什么不一样 · What makes it different
① 保真 80–95%：你的语序、垫词（呢 / 啊 / 对吧）、口头禅，全保留。
② 一个叫「重喷」的进化机制（核心）：
① 你口喷  →  ② AI 出稿  →  ③ 你对着稿子重念一遍  →  ④ AI 对比差异，重出稿
为什么「重喷」最关键？ AI 写的稿是"猜你"，你重念的那一版才是"真的你"。差异在哪儿，你的风格就在哪儿。 用得越久，越像你。
① 80–95% fidelity: your word order, filler words, catchphrases — all kept.
② The "Re-speak" loop (the core):
Why Re-speak matters most: the AI's draft is a guess; your re-read is the truth. The difference between them is your style. The more you use it, the more it sounds like you.
